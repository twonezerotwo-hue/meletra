Everything is transcribed and prepared on your own computer, without internet. Your audio, video, PDFs and pictures never leave it.

Sharing completed transcripts (off by default)

MELETRA shares nothing unless you switch it on. If you want to help improve transcription quality, you can turn on "Share completed transcript text with MELETRA" in Settings -> Privacy & Data; you are shown what would be shared before it starts.

What would be sent, only after the transcript has been saved on your computer: the transcript text (and the medical corrected transcript), the lecture name, the languages, the program version, a random installation number that identifies no person, and the time. What is never sent: audio, video, PDF files, pictures, file paths, your user name, your computer's name or any hardware number.

If you turn sharing off in Settings -> Privacy & Data, nothing is sent from then on and anything waiting to be sent is deleted. If the connection fails the program simply tries again later; transcribing is never delayed by it.
